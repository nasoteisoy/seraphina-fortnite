# Seraphina — Fortnite style (v3)

Play: https://nasoteisoy.github.io/seraphina-fortnite/ (works on phone)
Older v2: https://nasoteisoy.github.io/seraphina-fortnite/v2/

v3 polish:
- Roll: cape is pinned to her back during the roll and eases back to free motion afterwards (no flip-up).
- Bow lies flat and diagonal on her back, following the spine with a slight lag; cape is kept under it.
- Daggers are bigger in hand, leave a bright slash trail; hilts show in the hip sheaths.
- Arrow is drawn from the quiver, flies with a trail, sparks on hit and sticks in the target for a moment.
- Leftover bow/string slivers removed from the body mesh; patches where the bow touched the cape recoloured to the cape green.
- Game feel: hit flash + knockback, 3-tap dagger combo (●●● pips, spin finisher), bow aim assist, smoother camera,
  footstep dust + sounds, run/walk animation speed matched to ground speed (less foot sliding).

Phone: left stick to move, icon buttons: 🤸 roll, 🦶 kick, ⬆️ jump, 🔄 switch weapon, 🏹/🗡️ attack (tap 3× for the combo).
Desktop: WASD move, Shift run, Space jump, F attack, X/Tab switch, K kick, C roll.
